    Our story

    Made with ❤️

    In Hindi, a bhukkad is someone who just loves to eat. That's us, and that's you.

    Bhukkad Nama Café in Indore serves the food you grew up on, with a twist: hot parathas, dal tadka, steaming chai, cheesy maggi and momos, all at honest prices.

    Good food makes a good mood, so every plate is made fresh and served with a smile.
